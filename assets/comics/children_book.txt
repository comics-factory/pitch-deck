The Boy and the Magic Van
1. Chapter – The Secret in the Garage
Misi found an old, blue van in a dusty, cobweb‑filled garage. When he touched the steering wheel, the headlights suddenly lit up.
“Don’t be scared!” said a friendly voice. “I’m the Magic Van, and I’m longing for adventures.”

2. Chapter – The Dancing Camels of the Desert
The van lifted into the air, and in a moment they were floating above a hot desert.
When they reached a small town, Misi couldn’t believe his eyes: the camels were dancing to the beat of drums.
The locals taught him a desert dance step that made everyone burst out laughing.

3. Chapter – The Song of the Icy Mountain
Their next stop was a sparkling, icy mountain.
There, a group of penguins greeted Misi and sang a cold, yet beautiful song for him.
Misi hummed along, and even though he was off‑key, the penguins clapped for him.

4. Chapter – Among the Stars
At night, the van settled down on a soft, fluffy cloud to rest.
Misi lay on the roof of the van, watching the stars above.
“Thank you for this day,” he whispered.
“Tomorrow I’ll show you new worlds,” the van replied.
